FAQ

Questions, answered

▸ What is KOPI?

3,333 bean traders on BNB Chain. Six coffee varieties — Robusta, Arabica, Liberica, Excelsa, Luwak and the mythic Golden Bean — plus 100 Founders. Each mint is a random draw.

▸ How much does a mint cost?

Your bean is FREE — you only pay BNB gas. Max 1 bean per wallet. A Founder costs 0.001 BNB, max 1 per wallet, and only 100 exist.

▸ What does a Founder get?

First access to every future drop, plus the Founder badge on the NFT itself. Founders are the first 100 — once they are gone, they are gone.

▸ How does the marketplace work?

List your bean on this site — the NFT is held by the market contract (escrow) while listed. Buyers pay in BNB, the seller receives 95% and a 5% fee goes to the collection owner. You can cancel your listing any time to get your bean back.

▸ Is there a token?

No. KOPI is an NFT collection only — no token, no staking. Just the beans, the Founders and the market.
